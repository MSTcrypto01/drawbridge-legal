# Cryptocurrency Risk Disclosure

**DRAFT — not yet reviewed by qualified counsel.**

Crypto assets are high-risk. You could lose all the money you put into them. This is not financial advice.

## Key risks
- **Volatility**: prices can move sharply and unpredictably, including to zero.
- **Irreversibility**: confirmed transactions cannot be cancelled or reversed. Sending to a wrong address or network usually means permanent loss.
- **No protection schemes**: crypto assets are generally not covered by deposit insurance or investor compensation schemes. Crypto assets are not legal tender in most countries, including Saudi Arabia.
- **Scams and fraud**: impersonation, fake support, phishing sites, fake tokens using real names, "address poisoning" and malicious approvals are common. Drawbridge warns about some patterns but cannot detect every scam.
- **Technology**: blockchains, smart contracts, bridges, Layer-2 networks and stablecoins can fail, be exploited, be censored, fork, or lose their peg.
- **Network fees**: fees can rise suddenly; failed transactions can still cost fees.
- **Regulation and tax**: laws differ by country and change often. Some services may become unavailable to you. You are responsible for any taxes and reporting.
- **Liquidity**: you may not be able to sell an asset at the price you expect, or at all.
- **Test networks**: test coins have no value.

## Our software
Drawbridge is pre-release software. It has not yet completed independent security audits. Use small amounts and test networks while testing.

## Only use money you can afford to lose
Consider your circumstances and seek independent professional advice where appropriate.
