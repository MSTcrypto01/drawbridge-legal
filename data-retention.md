# Data Retention Policy

**DRAFT — not yet reviewed by qualified counsel.**

## On your device
| Data | Retention |
|---|---|
| Encrypted vault and authentication secrets | Until you remove the wallet or erase the device |
| Cached balances, prices, transaction records, address book | Until you erase the device |
| Portfolio value snapshots | Last 365 snapshots |
| Network activity log | Last 1,000 entries; clearable at any time |
| Consent records | Until you erase the device (exportable on request) |

## On Drawbridge systems
The basic wallet stores **no** user data on our systems. If optional services are introduced:
- operational logs: up to 30 days, without wallet addresses where possible;
- revenue and settlement accounting records (company records, not user profiles): as long as required by accounting and tax law (for example, up to 10 years);
- support and complaint records: up to 3 years after closure, unless law requires longer.

Data is deleted or anonymized at the end of its retention period.
