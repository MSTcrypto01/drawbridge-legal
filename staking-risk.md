# Staking Risk Disclosure

**DRAFT — staking is not offered in this build.**

If staking is offered in the future through third-party validators or liquid-staking protocols:
- **Rewards are not guaranteed** and vary with network conditions; displayed rates are estimates.
- **Lock-ups and unbonding periods** may prevent you from withdrawing or selling for days or weeks.
- **Slashing**: validator misbehavior or downtime can reduce your staked balance.
- **Liquid staking tokens** may trade below the value of the underlying asset and carry smart-contract risk.
- **Commissions**: validators charge a commission on rewards. Any Drawbridge commission will be disclosed as a separate percentage before you stake.
- Staking may be treated as a regulated activity in some jurisdictions and may be unavailable to you.
