# Regulatory Restrictions

**DRAFT — jurisdiction-by-jurisdiction review by qualified counsel is required before any public release.**

Drawbridge is self-custodial software: it does not hold customer assets or keys, exchange assets, or transmit money on users' behalf. Optional services (swaps, fiat on/off-ramps, staking, bridges) may be regulated and will be offered only through appropriately licensed partners, only where permitted, and may be restricted by country.

## Not available
The App must not be used where use of crypto assets or crypto wallets is prohibited, by persons subject to sanctions, or in comprehensively sanctioned jurisdictions.

## Country notes (to be validated by counsel)
- **Saudi Arabia**: no licensing regime for retail crypto intermediaries; SAMA has warned that virtual currencies are not legal tender. Drawbridge does not offer exchange, payment or fiat services in the Kingdom and does not market such services there.
- **Other GCC**: UAE licenses virtual-asset service providers (VARA, ADGM, DFSA, CBUAE/SCA regimes); Bahrain (CBB) licenses crypto-asset services; Kuwait prohibits virtual-asset activity; Qatar does not license retail crypto platforms; Oman applies registration and AML rules. Optional services will be geo-restricted accordingly.
- **European Union**: MiCA regulates crypto-asset service providers; non-custodial design alone does not automatically take a business out of scope, so any fee-bearing service will be assessed and offered only via authorized partners.
- **United Kingdom**: cryptoasset financial promotions are restricted; the FCA's full cryptoasset regime applies from 25 October 2027. Drawbridge will not promote regulated cryptoasset services to UK consumers without the required approvals.
- **United States**: FinCEN's 2019 guidance distinguishes unhosted wallet software from money transmitters; state laws and federal securities and commodities rules may apply to optional services.

## Your responsibility
You are responsible for complying with the laws of your country and for any taxes.
