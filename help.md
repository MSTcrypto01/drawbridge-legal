# Help and support

## Contact
A support email will be published here before public release. Until then, use the Help Center inside the app (it works offline).

When you write, describe the problem and the network involved. **Never send your recovery phrase, passphrase, PIN or
private keys** to anyone, including us.

## We will never
- ask for your recovery phrase, passphrase, PIN or private keys;
- ask you to install remote-access software or to move funds "for verification";
- contact you first through social media direct messages.

Anyone doing these things is impersonating Drawbridge.

## Common questions

**The app says Offline Vault. Is something wrong?**
No. Drawbridge makes no blockchain, price or Drawbridge-server connections until you open an online session. Tap
**Go online**, choose the networks and what the app may do, and the session ends on its own.

**A network shows "Couldn't refresh".**
Public endpoints sometimes limit requests. Drawbridge tries verified alternatives automatically; wait a minute and try
again. You can also use your own node in Settings → Endpoints (RPC).

**I lost my phone.**
Your funds are on the blockchains, not on the phone. Restore the wallet on a new device with your recovery phrase. If
someone else could unlock the old phone, move your funds to a new wallet.

**I lost my recovery phrase.**
Nobody can recover it, including us. If the app still opens, create a new wallet, write down its new phrase and move
your funds to it. Security → Recovery check confirms that a written phrase matches your wallet.

**I sent funds to the wrong address or network.**
Blockchain transactions cannot be reversed, and Drawbridge cannot recover them. Contact the recipient or the exchange
that controls the address.

**Why did I pay a fee?**
Network fees go to the blockchain's validators or miners. Drawbridge charges nothing to send, receive or store assets.
The review screen shows every fee before you approve.

**How do I practise without real money?**
Settings → Networks → turn on test networks, then open a session on Sepolia or Solana Devnet.

**Face ID does not work.**
Use the PIN you set during setup.

## Drawbridge Pro
- **Manage or cancel:** Settings → your name → Subscriptions on your iPhone. Cancel at least 24 hours before the end
  of the period to avoid the next renewal.
- **Restore a purchase:** open Drawbridge Pro and tap **Restore purchases**.
- **Refunds:** Apple handles payments and refunds; request one at
  [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com). Drawbridge never sees your payment details.
- Every security feature stays free.

## Report a security issue
If you find a vulnerability, contact us (see above) with "Security" in the subject and the steps to reproduce it. Test
only with your own wallets and test networks, and give us reasonable time to fix the issue before disclosing it. We
acknowledge reports within 2 business days.
