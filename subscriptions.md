# Subscription and Refund Policy

**DRAFT — Drawbridge Pro is configured for testing and is not yet on sale.**

## Drawbridge Pro
- **Price:** US$7.99 per month or US$59.99 per year (reference US prices). The App always shows the price the App Store sets for your region, including applicable taxes, before you buy.
- **What it adds:** portfolio analytics computed on your device; a consolidated accounting report with fees itemised; and 50% off Drawbridge swap and bridge fees where those services are offered. If swaps are not offered in your region, the App says so on the purchase screen.
- **What stays free:** creating and recovering wallets, sending, receiving, Offline Vault, transaction review and risk warnings, approval revocation, hardware-key protection, history export and every security feature. Paid features are never required to access, back up or move your assets.

## Billing
- Sold only through Apple In-App Purchase. Drawbridge never receives your payment details or Apple ID.
- Subscriptions renew automatically at the end of each period unless cancelled at least 24 hours before it ends, in Settings › Apple ID › Subscriptions.
- There is no free trial and no introductory price. Any future offer will be shown with its full terms before purchase.
- Price changes follow Apple's rules, including advance notice and, where required, your consent.

## Refunds
Refunds are handled by Apple under its policies (reportaproblem.apple.com). If Apple refunds a subscription, Pro features end. Statutory rights in your country are not affected.
