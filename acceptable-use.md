# Acceptable Use Policy

**DRAFT — not yet reviewed by qualified counsel.**

You must not use Drawbridge or any optional Drawbridge service to:
- violate any law or regulation, including anti-money-laundering, counter-terrorist-financing, sanctions, tax, consumer-protection or securities laws;
- deal with sanctioned persons, entities or jurisdictions, or evade sanctions;
- commit fraud, run scams, impersonate others, or distribute phishing or malware;
- finance illegal goods or services;
- interfere with, overload, or attempt to gain unauthorized access to any Drawbridge service or third-party endpoint;
- reverse engineer the App to harm users or bypass its security controls (good-faith security research under our disclosure policy is welcome);
- infringe intellectual-property or privacy rights.

Because Drawbridge is self-custodial software, we cannot freeze or seize assets on public blockchains. We may restrict optional services, cooperate with lawful requests to the extent we hold relevant data (which, by design, is minimal), and report as required by law.
